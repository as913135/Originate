# 🚀 ORIGINATE – Smart AI Business Advisory & FinTech for Bharat

## 📌 Problem Statement

Millions of aspiring entrepreneurs in India struggle to understand the complex legal, regulatory, financial, testing, accounting, and compliance requirements needed to start and run a business. Information is fragmented across multiple government portals, consultants, and departments, making the journey confusing, expensive, and time-consuming.

---

## 💡 Solution

**ORIGINATE** is an AI-powered multilingual business advisory platform that transforms business ideas into legally compliant and executable ventures.

The platform provides industry-specific guidance, government scheme discovery, compliance roadmaps, licensing assistance, and access to verified experts, enabling entrepreneurs to launch businesses with confidence.

## 🏗️ How It Works

1. User selects business category.
2. AI analyzes industry requirements.
3. Platform generates compliance roadmap.
4. User receives registration and licensing guidance.
5. Funding and subsidy eligibility is checked.
6. Compliance calendar is generated.
7. Verified experts can be consulted for execution.
